1What we collect

Lead Engine collects only the information we need to provide our services to you:

  • Information you submit: company name, your name and title, work email or WhatsApp number, main product category, target market, and competitors (optional)
  • Information generated automatically: access logs and basic analytics (page views, referral source, device type), used to improve the website

We do not collect sensitive personal information such as government ID numbers, bank account details or biometric data.

2How we use it

  • To prepare the AI Visibility Audit Report you requested
  • To discuss the audit results with you and confirm your target category and competitor list
  • With your consent, to propose follow-up services

We never sell or rent your information, or share it with third parties for marketing purposes.

3Your rights (including GDPR)

If you are in the EU or the European Economic Area, you have the following rights under the GDPR. We also comply with China's Personal Information Protection Law (PIPL) and extend the same rights to anyone it covers.

  • Right to be informed and right of access: know what information we hold about you and request a copy
  • Right to rectification: ask us to correct inaccurate information
  • Right to erasure (right to be forgotten): ask us to delete your information
  • Right to restrict processing and right to object: ask us to stop or limit processing
  • Right to data portability: receive your data in a commonly used format
  • Right to withdraw consent: at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of processing before you withdrew it

To exercise any of these rights, email sales@ileadengine.com. We respond within 30 days.

4Cookies and analytics

This site uses two types of cookies:

  • Essential cookies: keep the site's basic functions and security working. These can't be turned off.
  • Analytics cookies: we use a standard web analytics service (Google Analytics) to count visits anonymously, with IP anonymization, and improve our content. The script loads only after you click "Accept all." If you choose "Essential only," it is not loaded at all and no analytics cookies are set.

This site does not use advertising trackers or cross-site behavioral tracking cookies. You can also clear or block cookies in your browser settings at any time.

5Retention and security

Audit-related information is kept for 24 months from our last contact with you and then deleted. You can ask us to delete it sooner at any time.

The entire site is served over HTTPS. Form submissions are stored with Netlify (United States), and access is limited to the Lead Engine staff who need it.
