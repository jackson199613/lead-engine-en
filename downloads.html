A note on format

Why no PDFs?

Because AI crawlers often can't fetch PDF content, and when they do, they misread it. That is the first problem we fix for clients.

We ask clients to move their technical specs out of PDFs and into HTML. Locking our own material in PDFs would contradict that. If you need a PDF copy to circulate internally, let us know and we'll send one.
