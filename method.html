The GEO method

AI doesn't rank pages. It repeats facts it trusts.

A generative AI model gives an answer, not an ad: before it names a supplier, it needs facts it can verify, cross-check against other sources and quote in structured form. To be recommended, you have to become that kind of source, and that is the fundamental difference between GEO and traditional SEO.

GEO vs SEO

A different arena with different rules

In search, the buyer does the comparing. In an AI answer, the model has already compared for them and kept a few names. Your job shifts from winning a click to making the cut.

DimensionTraditional SEOGEO (Generative Engine Optimization)
GoalRank high in a list of resultsBe named and cited inside the generated answer
Unit of competitionKeywords and pagesFacts, parameters and entity relationships
Content formatMarketing copy written for peopleStructured technical source content written for models
Source of trustNumber and authority of backlinksConsistency across multiple independent sources
MetricsRankings, click-through rateMention rate, recommendation position, cited sources
Three core capabilities

What we actually do

Before AI names a supplier, three things have to be true: it can read your facts, it can confirm them somewhere else, and it can connect them to the buyer's exact question. Each capability below handles one of those.

1 · Readable

Semantic restructuring and Schema markup

We add Organization, Product, TechArticle, FAQPage and other structured data markup to your website, and release the key selection parameters in your TDS and COA documents from PDFs into machine-readable fields. Every category has different critical fields, so we first agree with your technical lead on exactly which ones drive a buyer's decision.

  • Product entities and parameter fields modeled
  • llms.txt and AI crawler access checked
  • Technical documents converted to indexable HTML
2 · Verifiable

Authoritative content seeding and external cross-verification

Models are cautious about what a single source says about itself, but they trust a fact that several independent sources state consistently. We systematically publish expert-level content in industry technical media, professional communities, LinkedIn and document platforms.

  • Scheduled long-form technical and application articles
  • Entries on industry platforms and in databases
  • White papers and selection guides
3 · Relevant

Competitor-model mapping and the "alternative to" strategy

We build a spec-by-spec mapping between your grades and the international big-brand models they compete with. When a buyer asks AI for "an alternative to model X," the model then has evidence to put your grade forward as a candidate.

  • Competitor-model parameter mapping library
  • Content for each substitution scenario
  • Application × system × recommended grade selection table
