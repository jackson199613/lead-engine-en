Common questions

Investment and return

Do AI recommendations actually lead to orders?

AI gives answers, not ads. Buyers trust you because they trust the AI's technical judgment, and they arrive having already done a first round of technical screening, so they convert noticeably better than paid-search traffic. Across the clients we track, AI-sourced inquiries show about 2.8× higher inquiry intent on average.

Why an annual plan instead of paying per task?

AI models weigh their recommendations on continuous multi-source cross-verification. A one-off push fades as the source content ages. An annual plan keeps the content updated and re-tested on schedule, and makes the results trackable and attributable.

How soon will I see results?

Within 2–6 weeks of the structured data going live, you can usually see AI repeat your specs more accurately. Significant changes in mention rate typically show up in months 2–4, depending on how competitive your category is and how much source content you already have.

I already have an SEO agency. Will this conflict?

No, the two are complementary. The structured data and technical content GEO relies on also improve traditional search performance. We can work directly with your existing SEO or web team and agree who does what.

How is my quote worked out?

Pricing is on request, because scope varies widely. We quote after the free audit, based on how many markets and languages you need, the size of your product range, and where you stand in AI answers today. GEO plans are billed annually. If you add the outbound line, it is billed monthly with a one-time setup, and the GEO plan is 30% off in a bundle. Ask for a quote.
