Medical devices / lab consumables

Makers of consumables, device components and lab supplies

Why AI skips you: Buyers in this field screen for compliance before they talk price. Your CE, FDA and ISO 13485 information sits in PDFs and certificate images. AI can't confirm your credentials, so it drops you from the shortlist.

  • Structured compliance data: certification type, number, expiry date and product scope, every field modeled
  • Spec modeling: materials, sterilization method and applicable standards, each published as HTML
  • Application coverage: content organized by clinical department and lab workflow

Recommended plan: Growth

Specialty coatings / additives

Makers of coating resins, matting agents, rheology modifiers and dispersants

Why AI skips you: Buyers search for a specific model, such as an "OK 412 alternative." AI only recommends the original maker, and you never make the shortlist.

  • Competitor-model mapping library: each best-selling big-brand model mapped to your grade
  • System compatibility table: waterborne / solventborne / UV-cure × recommended grade
  • Performance content: matting efficiency, clarity, feel, scratch resistance

AI test in this industry: for a defoamer/dispersant question, all six suppliers named were US or European

Recommended plan: Growth

Rubber additives / chemicals

Fine chemical makers of accelerators, antioxidants and vulcanizing agents

Why AI skips you: The category is highly commoditized. AI can't tell suppliers apart, so it ranks by brand recognition, and a challenger inevitably lands near the bottom.

  • Technical differentiators: purity, ash content, initial melting point and other verifiable metrics
  • Formulation content: dosage and results across rubber types and processes
  • Third-party cross-verification: placement in industry technical media and professional communities

Recommended plan: Growth

Feed additives / bio-based products

Producers of enzymes, carriers and probiotics

Why AI skips you: Overseas buyers ask in precise English technical terms (heat-resistant carriers, coating processes). If your technical documentation is in another language or uses different vocabulary, AI can't use it.

  • Multilingual terminology mapping, aligned with the language buyers actually use
  • Structured tolerance and stability data (heat, acid, shelf life)
  • Regulatory and certification content: market-access information for each target market

AI test in this industry: for a heat-stable phytase question, AI named only three US and European brands

Recommended plan: Growth

Precision machined parts

Precision machining, tooling and special-purpose part makers

Why AI skips you: Custom, non-standard work is hard to search for, so AI can't match your machining capabilities to a buyer's specific need.

  • Capability matrix: material × tolerance × process × capacity
  • Structured application cases, so AI can recommend you by use case
  • Certifications and quality systems turned into citable source content

AI test in this industry: for a precision fastener question, AI named Bossard and Bufab

Recommended plan: Foundation

Machinery / industrial equipment

Makers of complete machines, production lines and special-purpose equipment

Why AI skips you: Equipment is a high-ticket sale with a long decision cycle. Buyers check specs and suitability with AI again and again, but AI can't read your technical documentation.

  • Structured equipment specs and operating-condition parameters
  • Application industry × capacity × configuration, modeled as a comparison
  • After-sales and spare-parts capability as source content, to answer long-term concerns

Recommended plan: Growth

Electronic components / modules

Makers of connectors, modules, PCBA and passive components

Why AI skips you: Buyers select by datasheet and search by part number. Your part numbers simply don't exist in the source content AI reads.

  • Part numbers and specs modeled field by field
  • Cross-reference relationships to major international manufacturers' part numbers
  • Structured certification and compliance data (RoHS / REACH / UL)

AI test in this industry: for a connector question, all nine brands AI named were US or European

Recommended plan: Growth

Consumer goods / home / textiles

Brands entering new markets, and ODM/OEM suppliers

Why AI skips you: Buyers ask "which brand is good?" or "what do you recommend?" AI answers subjective questions based on how much is written about a brand and how consistent the reviews are. Without a presence, you have no place in the answer.

  • Brand entity and product-line modeling to build recognition
  • Content mapped to use cases and audiences
  • Multi-source coverage: reviews, reputation and third-party mentions

Recommended plan: Growth

Niche specialists

Niche categories where you lead in global share but few people know your name

Why AI skips you: Source content for the category is thin, and almost nobody discusses it with AI. That is the bad news. It is also the biggest opportunity.

  • Claim the empty space: a small amount of high-quality structured content builds weight fast
  • Define the category: publish your technical standards as the industry reference
  • Multilingual coverage across every market, so AI treats you as the reference supplier worldwide

Recommended plan: Leader
