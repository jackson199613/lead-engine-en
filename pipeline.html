Outbound line

The Bionic SDR

An AI-assisted outbound system modeled on a seasoned export sales rep. Find, research, write and follow up, four stages working together. What we report is a verifiable count of inquiries with their grades, not a send-volume report.

  1. 1

    Find

    Filter target companies and contacts by category, market, company profile and buying committee

    Standard we holdMulti-source cross-verification; real companies and real decision-makers first

  2. 2

    Research

    Build a 360° company profile: business, products, markets and buying signals

    Standard we holdDeep account research in 10 minutes; every email uses at least 2 research facts

  3. 3

    Write

    Assemble each email from subject, opener, value, proof and ask structures, one email at a time

    Standard we hold30,720 structure combinations; no subject/opener combination repeats on the same domain within 60 days

  4. 4

    Follow up

    Send Monday to Friday in the buyer's time zone, five-touch follow-up, read reply intent and grade it

    Standard we holdDelivery and bounces monitored continuously to protect your sender domains; replies go to your sales dashboard as A/B/C

How GEO runs

From audit to a measurable citation share

Content engineering

Authority signals

Technical documents, industry platforms and third-party mentions that make you credible to AI.

Monitor and optimize

Monthly tracking of your citation share in ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and AI Overviews.

The deliverable is a measurable citation share, not a feeling that rankings went up. We don't mass-distribute press releases or fabricate content. GEO is built only on your real, verifiable specs, certifications and supply capability, in line with our published quality red lines.

Output comparison

The same outreach job, 10× the output

An experienced rep can research and write 10–15 personalized emails a day. The pipeline does the same research for every email, from about 1,000 a day. And it doesn't get tired, quit or need a pep talk.

Human

Two in-house sales reps

  • Payroll from day one: salary, commission and benefits for two people, before the first inquiry
  • 20–30 emails a day between them: by the eighth email it's copy-paste, and research runs on gut feel
  • Need managing: onboarding, coaching, chasing and retention often cost more than the salaries suggest
  • No sends on weekends or holidays: or during sick leave, vacation, or an off week
  • 6–12 months before they close on their own: and when someone leaves, their accounts and scripts leave too
Machine

One inquiry pipeline

  • Fee: custom quote (Standard plan): scoped to your markets and volume; plan changes take effect the same month
  • About 1,000 emails a day and up: email 20,000 gets the same research and personalization as email 1
  • No morale to manage: no meetings, pep talks or retention plans
  • Sends Monday to Friday in the buyer's time zone: no sick days, no resignations, no breaks
  • Scripts and account data stay in the system: if a rep leaves, the pipeline keeps running

Lead grading and handover

What reaches your team is already graded

We grade each inquiry on how clear the need is, whether the company is real, and the contact's buying role, so your sales team works the ones they can quote, sample or move forward first.

GradeDefinitionRecommended sales action
AA-grade Real company, a buying or decision-making role, a clear requirement. Ready to quote or send samples. Top of the sales dashboard: quote, sample, negotiate
BB-grade Buying interest, but specs, volume, timing or decision chain still need confirming Your rep confirms the requirement and moves it forward
CC-grade Genuine interest, but no defined buying window yet Nurture and follow up
