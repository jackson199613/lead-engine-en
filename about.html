What we do

We build the digital assets that get manufacturers recommended by AI

For twenty years, export manufacturers found buyers through trade shows, B2B marketplaces and paid search. Today the buyer's journey starts with a question to an AI assistant, and the default answer often lists only a handful of legacy incumbents from the US, Europe and Japan.

Lead Engine exists to translate a challenger manufacturer's real technical strength into a form AI can understand, is willing to cite and can confidently recommend.

AI gives answers, not ads. To get into the answer, you first have to become a fact.

Why now

Why we started doing this

Because it happened to us first. In the second half of 2025, a customer replied to one of our emails: "We asked ChatGPT, and you didn't show up."

I didn't believe it, so I tested it with real buyer questions. Only then did I see how widespread the problem was. We later turned this into a public test. Asked for industrial connector suppliers, AI named nine brands, all US or European incumbents. Not one was a Chinese or other Asian manufacturer, even though China-based Luxshare Precision is one of the world's largest connector makers. Asked about solar PV instead, AI volunteered Chinese manufacturers JinkoSolar, LONGi and Trina Solar. AI doesn't pick suppliers by origin. The gap wasn't the product. It was the source content.

These results come from our test on August 12, 2026. The exact questions and screenshots are in Research & updates, so you can rerun them yourself.

We looked around and found almost no one doing this seriously for technical, spec-heavy products. So from day one, Lead Engine has done only this. We are not a legacy marketing agency that pivoted.

FAQ

The four questions clients ask most

Do AI recommendations actually lead to orders?

AI gives answers, not ads. Buyers trust you because they trust the AI's technical judgment, and they arrive having already passed a first technical screen, so they convert noticeably better. Across the clients we track, inquiry intent is on average about 2.8× higher.

Other agencies offer GEO too. What makes Lead Engine different?

They know marketing. We know industrial products. GEO is not about writing beautifully. It is about writing content that is accurate, verifiable and consistent across independent sources. We produce source content on material parameters, process conditions and test standards, which AI more readily treats as expert fact. Get one digit of a spec wrong and AI won't cite you. Get it right and it cites you again and again.

What is the difference between GEO and SEO?

SEO competes for a ranking in a list of results. GEO competes to be named inside the generated answer. The unit of competition shifts from keywords and pages to facts, parameters and entity relationships, and the source of trust shifts from backlink authority to consistency across multiple independent sources.

Our product is very niche and nobody asks AI about it. Is it still worth doing?

Yes, often more so. The more niche the category, the thinner the source content AI has, and the easier it is to build weight with a small amount of high-quality structured content. Niche categories usually deliver the best return on GEO, and you may be the only company in your category doing it.
